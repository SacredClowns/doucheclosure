The Sketch

Long before anybody had a phone in their pocket, the entire evidentiary record of this phenomenon was witness drawings — regular people, handed a pencil by a man with a clipboard, and asked to render the impossible. The board has restored the practice. You will be shown something for two seconds. Then you will draw it. Your drawing will be entered into the permanent record, and it will be judged.

You Are The Instrument.

There is no camera in this room. There has never been a camera in this room. For a hundred years the instrument of record in this field was a person, a pencil, and about four minutes of nerve, and the board is not going to pretend otherwise.

Five objects. Each one is shown for two seconds. Then the sheet goes blank and you have thirty seconds to put down what you saw. You may undo. You may clear. You may not look again — nobody ever gets to look again.

The board scores by occupancy grid. The method is printed on the docket, in the open, because we are not animals.

WING DOCTRINE: We do not question the sighting. We question the intake procedure. A man sees something over a river and the entire apparatus of human knowledge hands him a pencil, a clipboard, and thirty seconds — and then spends fifty years reviewing his line quality. This cabinet puts you on the other side of the clipboard, where it turns out the object is genuinely very hard to draw and the pencil is genuinely not sharpened.
