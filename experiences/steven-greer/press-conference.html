Nine Thirty. They Are Actually Here.

Every one of them has something to lose by walking through that door and every one of them walked through it anyway. Your job today is not persuasion. It is sequencing.

Eight slots. Eighty-four minutes. Twelve witnesses who between them need a hundred and fifteen. You are going to cut people who drove here, and the ones you cut are not going to get another room like this, and you have to do it anyway, because a program that runs long is a program the wire services leave early.

The room will teach you the rules if you let it. Watch the meter. Watch the back table. Nobody in that press row is hostile — they are tired, which is worse.

WING DOCTRINE: This cabinet does not run on irony. In May 2001 a press conference at the National Press Club put roughly twenty named military, intelligence and aviation witnesses on the public record in a single afternoon — more than the previous fifty years had managed combined. Our grievance with this wing has always been the invoice: the folding chair, the tiered retreat pricing, the sky sold by the seat. It has never once been the witnesses, and it has never been that Wednesday. The twelve on tonight's roster are composites written for a game; the room they are standing in was real, and it cost the people in it something to be there. Play accordingly.
