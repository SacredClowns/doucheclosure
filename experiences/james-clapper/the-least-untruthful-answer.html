Doucheclosure Experiences • The Committee Room

The Least
Untruthful Answer

In March 2013 the Director of National Intelligence was asked, in open session, whether the NSA collects data on millions of Americans, and answered “No, sir… not wittingly.” He later said he had tried to answer in the “least untruthful manner,” and apologized in writing for an answer he called “clearly erroneous.” That paragraph is the entire documented record, and it is the only thing in this wing that is real. Everything below it is a committee-room comedy we built on top of it.

OPEN SESSION • THE ANSWER IS CLASSIFIED • THE QUESTION IS NOT

The review board wishes to state its position before anyone starts enjoying themselves. We are not here to call a man a liar. We are here to audit the room. A room was built in which a public official could be asked a public question whose true answer was, at that moment, a federal crime to say out loud. Then a microphone was placed in front of him. Then everyone acted surprised.

Somebody had to invent a unit of measurement on live television with no warning, no draft, and no lawyer within arm's reach. What came out was “least untruthful.” The board finds this to be, structurally, an engineering achievement. It is also going to be on his headstone, and everyone in that room knew it within four seconds.

So per doctrine we have built the only responsible thing: a hearing simulator where you are in the chair. Eight questions. One dial. Five positions, from FULLY TRUE to NO, SIR. Slide it and watch your own sentence rewrite itself in real time — watch it get shorter, cleaner, safer, and emptier, one detent at a time. Then survive the part nobody in that room got to skip: three months later, when the documents come out and the transcript is still sitting there, in bold, with your adverb circled by hand.

The Adverb Audit

Line itemReview board notes
THE ADVERB“Wittingly.” Board's note: one adverb performing the structural load of an entire building. The board attempted to remove it from the sentence in a controlled setting and the sentence collapsed. It is load-bearing. It should be inspected annually and no one should park under it.
THE FORUMThe question was asked in a room where the true answer could not lawfully be given. Board's finding: this is not a character test, it is a plumbing problem. You cannot push a classified answer through an unclassified door. Somebody specified that door. Somebody signed off on the door. The door did not testify.
THE UNIT“Least untruthful.” Board's note: a genuinely new unit of measurement, introduced under oath, with no conversion chart and no calibration standard, and now in permanent worldwide circulation. We have adopted it internally. It is on our expense forms. Line 4: LEAST UNTRUTHFUL MILEAGE.
THE LETTERA written correction, describing the answer as clearly erroneous. Board's audit of the logistics: postage, negligible. Transit time, weeks. Readership, approximately nobody. Board's finding: the correction traveled slower than the disclosure. It always does. That is the entire subject of this website.
CHAIN OF CUSTODYWho owns a sentence once it leaves the room? Board's ruling: the transcript does. Not the speaker, not the committee, not the context. And the transcript does not take context. The transcript takes dictation.
THE REAL COSTFiled sincerely and without a joke attached: a man was required to answer in public a question he was forbidden in public to answer, and the office that forbade him was not seated at that table and was never asked to explain the seating chart. We audit the room. We do not audit the witness.
WING DOCTRINE: The witness in the game below is THE DIRECTOR — a fictional composite. Every senator, every question, every answer, and every “subsequently disclosed” document in this game was invented by us for a game. No words in this simulator were ever spoken by any real person, with the sole exception of the three documented phrases quoted in the header above, which are matters of public record and are quoted as such. Nobody in this hearing is a villain. That is the joke: everyone at that table is doing their job correctly and the outcome is still a disaster.
