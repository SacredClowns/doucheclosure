The Hall Of Shrines

We roast them in the arcade • We book them in the hall

Downstairs, this organization makes fun of the paperwork. Up here, we do the other half of the job. Every person in this hall said a thing out loud that cost them something — a career, a reputation, a decade of being laughed at by people who have never risked a Tuesday. So each of them gets a shrine: the record of what they actually say, where to watch them, where to follow them, and — most importantly — how to book them. If you came here to laugh, stay to hire somebody.

House rule: equal-opportunity offender. Nobody in this field gets a pass — which is why the last shrine in this hall belongs to Doucheclosure Press, and it is the harshest one we published.

ONE WING HAS NO SHRINE. The Aggregator has no record to display, no clips of his own to watch, and nothing to book — a shrine requires a story, and that is the entire finding of Wing 10. The hall is for people who put their name on something.
