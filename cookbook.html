Declassified Sample — Recipe No. 1

First Contact Crab Cakes

The first thing they teach you in the secret culinary program they deny exists: when an entity reaches for you, reach for the Old Bay.

  • 1 lb lump crab-person meat, picked over for shell fragments and dog tags (civilian sub: jumbo lump crab)
  • 1 egg • ¼ cup mayo • 1 tsp Dijon • 1 tsp Worcestershire
  • 1½ tsp Old Bay • ½ cup crushed saltines • chives • butter • lemon

Form six plump cakes. Chill 30 minutes so they hold together under interrogation. Sear in butter 3–4 minutes per side. Serve with lemon and total deniability.

Field note: If your cakes fall apart, they were compromised from within. Add one more tablespoon of mayo and trust no one.
